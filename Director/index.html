Trail station signs

Generates 6 stations with unique, non-repeating calls spread evenly across B-I-N-G-O. Stations lock in for this ride once generated — end the ride to start a new set.

Fewer calls per station makes patterns rarer (good for a big turnout, more cards in play). More calls per station raises everyone's odds (good for a small turnout). 12 is the practical maximum with 6 stations — beyond that you run out of fresh numbers to call.
